Practical details

Before you start.

Can you handle large event orders?

Yes. WORK+SHELTER supports promotional-product programs and fully custom manufacturing, with planning based on quantity, complexity, destinations, and event date.

How early should we start?

Earlier gives you more creative freedom. Promotional projects can typically run around 2–10 weeks, while fully custom manufacturing generally needs 8–16 weeks.

Can you create different tiers for attendees, speakers, VIPs, and staff?

Yes. A single event can use multiple product tiers while keeping a coherent visual and impact story.

Can sponsor branding be incorporated?

Yes. Depending on the product, branding can be integrated through print, embroidery, labels, tags, packaging, inserts, and other custom details.

Can you help communicate the impact?

Yes. Custom tags, inserts, packaging, and factual impact language can explain where and how the products were made and support event recaps, sponsor reports, and impact communications.

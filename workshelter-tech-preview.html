Practical details

Before you start.

How custom can the product be?

WORK+SHELTER supports ready-to-customize catalog goods as well as custom-sewn production, including fabric sourcing, custom colors, print, embroidery, labels, hardware, patternmaking, grading, and specialty packaging.

What are the minimums?

Promotional products generally begin at 100 units and a $1,000 minimum order. Custom manufacturing begins at $2,500 per style.

How long does production take?

Timing depends on the service level and complexity. WORK+SHELTER currently lists roughly 2–10 weeks for promotional products and 8–16 weeks for custom manufacturing.

Can WORK+SHELTER help with the impact story?

Yes. The product can incorporate custom hang tags, inserts, branded labels, and packaging. We can also help think through the factual story behind the production so the impact feels connected to the product rather than pasted onto it.
